# superstore-sales-analysis

An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes sales performance, compares year-over-year results, and uncovers product-level insights to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Project Summary

Businesses generate large volumes of sales data, but without effective analysis, it can be difficult to identify trends, measure performance, and make informed decisions. This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset and presents key business metrics through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

The dashboard enables users to monitor sales performance, evaluate profitability, compare current and previous year performance, and identify trends across products and categories.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project was to:

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze sales and profit trends.
- Compare current year performance against the previous year.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming product subcategories.
- Support data-driven business decision-making through interactive visualizations.

---

## Tools Used

- Tableau

This project uses the **Sample Superstore** dataset, a publicly available retail sales dataset commonly used for business intelligence and data visualization projects.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for **Total Sales**, **Total Profit**, and **Total Quantity**.
- Year-over-Year (Current Year vs Previous Year) performance comparison.
- Monthly sales and profit trend analysis.
- Product subcategory comparison for sales and profitability.
- Quantity analysis by product category.
- Interactive year filter for dynamic analysis.

---

## Key Insights

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall sales, profit, and quantity performance.
- Compare current year performance with the previous year.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming product subcategories.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Explore quantity distribution across product categories.
